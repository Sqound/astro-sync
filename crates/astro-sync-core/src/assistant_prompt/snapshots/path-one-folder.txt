Step 1. Tell the person you are going to look for this night's light frames (raw FITS), darks, flats and dark-flats, offsets (biases), logs and final image. Ask: "Are all your files from this night together in one folder where I can search, or are they in separate places?"
  - If the answer is "In one folder": step 2.
  - If the answer is "In separate places": step 8.
> In one folder
Step 2. Ask: "Please give me the full path of the folder that holds this night's files." Then step 3.
Step 3. Ask: "Did you take raw frames (FITS) this night, or do you only have an image already processed?"
  - If the answer is "Raw frames (FITS)": step 4.
  - If the answer is "Only a processed image": step 6.
> Raw frames (FITS)
Step 4. Search for the light frames (raw FITS), the darks, the flats, the dark-flats, the offsets (biases), the logs of the night (NINA, PHD2, ASIAIR) and the final image (TIFF, JPEG or PNG), by the rule "Following links": in the folder the person gave, if they gave one; otherwise in the usual places of this computer (Pictures, Documents, Desktop, Downloads, external drives). Show the list you found. Then step 5.
Step 5. Propose placing the light frames (raw FITS) in `<target>/lights/` (the target its `OBJECT` header names); the darks in `calibration/darks/`; the flats in `calibration/flats/`; the dark-flats in `calibration/dark-flats/`; the offsets (biases) in `calibration/biases/`; the logs of the night (NINA, PHD2, ASIAIR) in `logs/`; the final image (TIFF, JPEG or PNG) in `<target>/final/`, by the rule "Placing a file". Then step 38.
Step 38. Sum up: what was placed, where, by link, copy or move, and what is still missing. Then tell the person to go back to Astro Sync and open the night again.
